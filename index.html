🌿 Cedar Park Family Medicine

Family medicine with a receptionist who never puts you on hold.

Sage, our AI receptionist, handles bookings, refill requests, and triage 24/7 — in your browser, no phone tag, no waiting room queue.

Click the button to start. Sage will ask for your name and date of birth before booking — that's the HIPAA identity gate at work.

Why no phone number? The Bright Smile Dental demo uses a Vapi-provisioned US phone number (routed through Twilio). For Cedar Park, every component that touches voice data is a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement decision — fewer components, fewer ways to get it wrong. Production deployment re-introduces a BAA-covered Twilio plan.

How a call flows

From hello to confirmed appointment in about 75 seconds — identity verification included.

1

You start a call

Click "Talk to Sage" — voice streams over WebRTC to a HIPAA-aware GPT-4.1 agent.

2

Identity gate

Sage asks for your name and date of birth before any tool call — required before confirming "patient" status.

3

Booked or routed

Appointments land in our calendar. Refill requests and triage route to the clinical team via the secure message queue.

What Sage handles

Five flows, all enforced by the same identity-verification gate.

New patient intake

Demographics, insurance, chief complaint, new-patient visit scheduling.

Existing patient booking

Identity verification, then follow-up or annual scheduling.

Insurance verification

Plan capture and hand-off to the billing team for eligibility check.

Refill requests

Identity + medication + pharmacy, routed via the secure message queue.

Urgent triage

Same-day visit vs. dial-911 escalation — distinguished by a word-for-word script.

Hours & FAQ

Location, hours, accepted insurance — no PHI required.
